# Meridian — publication and advertising notes

Prepared 26 September 2026. Internal implementation notes, not customer terms or a legal opinion.

## Items to resolve before using the draft

1. Supply the operator's full legal name, address, support email, production URL, registration details if applicable, and intended countries of service. Confirm South Africa as governing law or replace the provisional clause. Do not publish placeholders.
2. Ask a South African lawyer experienced in financial services and consumer law to assess the actual AI entry/TP/SL functionality under FAIS and any other applicable regulation. An educational label or a disclaimer does not decide whether an activity is regulated. Do not invent a licence number or assert exemption.
3. Review the liability clauses for the actual business model and audience. Consider CPA applicability, fairness, prominent notices and non-excludable obligations. A checkbox does not automatically make an unfair clause enforceable.
4. Publish a separate accurate Privacy Notice. Inventory screenshot/context transmission, Groq processing, Render hosting/logs, Twelve Data calls, browser storage, retention, international transfers, lawful grounds, data-subject requests, contacts and any advertising providers. Verify vendor settings and contracts before promising deletion or no retention. The current UI's statement that Meridian does not save images must not imply that vendors keep no records.
5. Confirm Twelve Data display and redistribution rights for the actual group/public audience. Public hosting does not create these rights.
6. Implement accessible footer links and a deliberate acceptance step where appropriate, before first analysis. Display the risk notice prominently. Record the exact terms version, acceptance timestamp and relevant evidence proportionately, with a privacy basis and retention period. A browser-only flag is easy to clear and is not a robust account acceptance record. Do not bundle optional tracking consent into terms acceptance.
7. Confirm an 18+ audience policy and its practical implementation. The draft proposes this rule; the current app does not enforce it.
8. Keep complaints contact details monitored. Retain versioned copies of published terms and announce material changes prospectively.

## Advertising next milestone

Google Ads is generally used to buy advertising for your business. AdSense is the publisher product generally used to show ads on your own site. Confirm whether you want to promote Meridian, monetise its pages, or both before integration.

Before serving ads, identify the network and visitor locations, publish the required privacy/cookie disclosures, and implement the relevant consent controls. For Google ad serving to users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, check Google's current certified CMP requirements. Do not load non-essential trackers before consent where consent is required. Provide a way to revisit choices. Do not claim advertising consent exists merely because someone accepted these Terms.

Clearly label ad placements, distinguish advertisements from setup results and analysis controls, and disclose affiliate relationships. Avoid accidental-click layouts and claims of guaranteed trading profits. Review the selected network's financial-content restrictions and advertiser requirements before launch. No ad integration, tracking scripts or advertising consent mechanism has been added in this task.
